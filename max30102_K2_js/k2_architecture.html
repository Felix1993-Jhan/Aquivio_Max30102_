調 LED 電流比較接近「重新校正硬體」,不是「調一個參數」。
不受影響(全是比值,分子分母同時縮放會抵銷):谷顯著度門檻、SQI 的 cv 與 spike、 以及找谷 → RR → HRV 整條線 —— 它走的是去趨勢後的相對形狀,不看絕對位準。 換句話說 心率與 HRV 對 LED 電流是穩健的。
會受影響:
· fingerThreshold —— 直接與 IR DC 比,同單位。電流拉高 DC 跟著漲,門檻變得太好過;調低則可能永遠達不到。必須跟著重調。
· SpO2 —— 血氧多項式的係數是針對特定光學條件校出來的。改變紅光/紅外的電流比例會讓 R 系統性偏移,而公式照舊算 → 血氧穩定地偏掉幾個百分點,看起來完全正常、沒有任何異常徵兆。
· ADC 飽和 —— IR 是 18-bit(上限 262143)。電流過大會削平波峰、破壞 AC 幅度,SpO2 直接失效(波形上看得出頂部被切平)。 Changing LED current is closer to re-calibrating the hardware than tuning a parameter.
Unaffected (all ratios — numerator and denominator scale together): the prominence threshold, SQI cv and spike, and the whole trough → RR → HRV chain, which works on detrended shape rather than absolute level. In short, HR and HRV are robust to LED current.
Affected:
· fingerThreshold — compared directly against IR DC, same units. More current raises DC and the threshold becomes too easy; less current may make it unreachable. Must be re-tuned.
· SpO2 — the polynomial coefficients are calibrated for a specific optical condition. Changing the red/IR current ratio shifts R systematically while the formula stays the same → SpO2 drifts by a few percent, looking entirely normal with no visible symptom.
· ADC saturation — IR is 18-bit (max 262143). Too much current clips the peaks and destroys the AC amplitude, breaking SpO2 outright (visible as a flat-topped waveform).
輪詢間隔不得超過 240 ms。MCU 端一次最多交出 24 組樣本 (封包上限 151 = 7 + 24×6),100 Hz 下就是 240 ms 的量。超過這個間隔,較舊的樣本會被覆蓋掉。
掉樣本不會報錯 —— 波形看起來仍然連續(丟的是中間那段),但 RR 會偏長、心率偏低。 模組也偵測不到:它只看到「這批 10 筆」,不知道 MCU 那邊有幾筆被蓋掉了。 預設 100 ms 有 2.4 倍餘裕。 Do not poll slower than 240 ms. The MCU returns at most 24 samples per response (packet cap 151 = 7 + 24×6), which is 240 ms at 100 Hz. Poll any slower and older samples are overwritten.
Lost samples raise no error — the waveform still looks continuous (the missing span is in the middle), but RR drifts long and HR reads low. The module cannot detect it either: it only sees the 10 samples handed to it. The 100 ms default leaves 2.4× headroom.

CS = (0x100 − (前面所有 byte 相加 & 0xFF)) & 0xFF
驗證時更簡單:整包(含 CS)相加 & 0xFF 應為 0。上表每一組位元組都已依此算出(站號詳見 D 頁)。 Checksum. First two header bytes are 40 71, the third is the station (30/31), command is 09, and the last byte is the CS:
CS = (0x100 − (sum of all preceding bytes & 0xFF)) & 0xFF
Verification is simpler: the whole packet including CS must sum to 0 under & 0xFF. Every byte string above was computed this way.
